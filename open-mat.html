How It Works

Your Mat Time.

Open Mat sessions give wrestlers the freedom to drill, wrestle live, work specific positions, or train with their partners based on what they need most.

A coach will be available throughout the session for questions, technical feedback, and guidance. Depending on attendance and the needs of the group, sessions may also include short technique instruction or organized situational wrestling.

There is no advance registration required. Complete the online waiver before your first session, then show up ready to train.

$25 Single-session drop-in

Before You Train

Show Up & Wrestle.

No advance registration is required for Open Mat. Once your waiver is on file, simply come to the wrestling room on Monday night and train.
