Registration

How To Register.

To register for a clinic, contact us by phone, text, or email with the wrestler's name and the clinic you would like to attend. You may complete the required waiver ahead of time and send it by email or text, or bring a physical copy with you.

Payment can be made by cash, check made payable to Ryan Luth, Venmo (@ryan_luth), or Zelle (203-815-6699). A valid USA Wrestling membership is required. See below for the waiver and USA Wrestling membership links.

For the Bring-A-Friend Rate, both wrestlers must register together to receive the $40-per-wrestler rate. Walk-ins are accepted at the standard $80 registration rate if space is available.
