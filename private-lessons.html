Booking & Cancellation Policy

A 50% deposit is required to confirm your reservation. The remaining balance is due at the time of the lesson.

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled lesson will receive a full refund of the deposit.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled lesson, as well as no-shows, will forfeit the deposit.

Payment: Deposit can be made with Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal. Payment information will be provided once your lesson time is confirmed. The remaining balance may be paid at the time of the lesson.
