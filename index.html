Our Approach

Refine And Expand Your Game.

📍 Biddiscombe Wrestling Room · 161 Cross St, Middletown, CT

*Luth Wrestling Academy is independently operated and is not a program of Wesleyan University or Wesleyan Wrestling. Training sessions are held in the Biddiscombe Wrestling Room.

We specialize in providing focused, individualized, supplemental training in private lessons, small group sessions, and film review. Additionally, we plan to offer a weekly open-mat practice for wrestlers of all skill levels to have access to high-level coaching, and we will also begin running clinics on specific techniques and skill sets beginning in the Fall of 2026.

Our Coaches

Experience Matters.

Ryan Luth

Founder · Associate Head Wrestling Coach, Wesleyan University

Ryan Luth is a three-time NCAA Division III All-American from Washington and Lee University and currently serves as Associate Head Wrestling Coach at Wesleyan University.

As a collegiate wrestler, Ryan was a two-time regional champion, three-time conference champion, and finished his career with a 101–11 record.

Ryan has coached NCAA national qualifiers and All-Americans at both Washington and Lee and Wesleyan. Through individual training, he has also worked with Connecticut state champions and New England place-winners.
