Our Coaches

Ryan Luth

Associate Head Coach · Wesleyan University
3X All-American
101–11 Collegiate Record
220–19 High School Record

Ryan Luth is the Associate Head Coach at Wesleyan University. Prior to arriving at Wesleyan, he spent one season as an assistant coach at Washington and Lee University, his alma mater. Across three seasons of collegiate coaching, Coach Luth has helped develop four NCAA Division III national qualifiers, two All-Americans, and more than 10 regional placewinners. He has also worked privately with multiple Connecticut state champions, New England placewinners, and high school All-Americans.

As a collegiate wrestler at Washington and Lee, Luth was a three-time All-American at 157 pounds, placing fifth nationally in 2023, fourth in 2022, and earning First-Team All-America honors in 2020. He was also a two-time regional champion and three-time conference champion. Luth finished his collegiate career with a 101–11 record and was named Washington and Lee's Athlete of the Year in both 2022 and 2023. He also received the Pres Brown Most Valuable Senior Male Athlete Award and was a three-time NWCA Academic All-American.

A 2018 graduate of Joseph A. Foran High School, Luth finished his high school career with a 220–19 record, setting the Connecticut high school record for career wins. He was a three-time NHSCA All-American, two-time New England Champion, two-time State Open Champion, two-time Class M Champion, and four-time Southern Connecticut Conference Champion. He was named the State Open Most Outstanding Wrestler in 2017 and Class M Most Outstanding Wrestler in 2018 and earned Scholar All-American honors in all four years of high school.
